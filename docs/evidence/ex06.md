## SPF, DKIM, and DMARC

The Zoho message-list metadata available for this audit does not include receiving-mailbox `Authentication-Results` headers. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results for these messages are therefore **not verified**. Do not infer message authentication from domain DNS records or Zoho setup checks.
